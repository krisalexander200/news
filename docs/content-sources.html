Sources & licenses

NewsDrip helps you discover independently published news. We display headlines with source credits and links to the full articles. We do not reproduce article images or full article text, and we do not rewrite headlines with AI.

Global Voices

Eligible headlines are credited to the named author and Global Voices, with a link to the original article. Unless otherwise stated, Global Voices-created content is available under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. See the republishing guidelines. Guest and visibly restricted partner entries are excluded from this feed. Third-party images and media are not included.

Wikinews

Wikinews is now an archive. Articles older than 30 days are excluded from the current feed, so it currently contributes no headlines.

Headlines are credited to Wikinews contributors and linked to the original page. Text created from December 16, 2024 uses Creative Commons Attribution 4.0; earlier eligible text from September 25, 2005 uses Attribution 2.5. Each story shows its applicable license. See the copyright policy.

How content is used

Headline wording is preserved. Feed markup is converted to plain text for display. Source attribution and license links remain visible in both display modes. Licensed article excerpts and photographs are not displayed. Publisher links open the original website, whose privacy policy and access conditions apply.

Publishers and authors do not endorse NewsDrip. Names identify the original sources, not a partnership. NewsDrip does not claim ownership of their work.
